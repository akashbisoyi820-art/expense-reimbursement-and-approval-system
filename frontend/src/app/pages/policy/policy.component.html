How the rules work

  • Per-claim limit: a single claim above this amount is flagged Over limit for the manager. It is still submitted.
  • Monthly limit: if one employee's claims in a category in one month (rejected claims excluded) go above this amount, the claim is flagged.
  • Receipt required: claims above this amount need a receipt. The employee cannot submit without one, and a manager cannot approve until it is uploaded.
  • Changes apply to claims submitted after saving. Claims already submitted keep the flags they got at that time.
