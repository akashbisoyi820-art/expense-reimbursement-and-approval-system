Policy engine

Rules that guide, not block

Admins set the limits for each category. A claim over a limit is flagged for the manager with a clear reason, and a required receipt is enforced, so exceptions stay visible.

  • Per-claim and monthly limits, and a receipt rule, for every category
  • Limits are checked on the INR amount, whatever the currency
  • Every policy change is kept in a history

Expense assistant

Answers from your own policy and claims

Ask about a limit, a receipt rule or the status of a claim. The assistant reads the real policy and your own claims. It is rule-based, so it never makes things up, and no data leaves the system.

  • Policy limits, receipt rules and how the process works
  • Whether a planned claim is within the limits
  • The status and the next step of any of your claims
Expense assistant
Can I claim 7500 for travel?
Travel allows up to ₹10,000 per claim, so 7,500 is within the limit. This month you have claimed ₹4,500, so the total would be ₹12,000, over the monthly limit of ₹30,000? No, it is within it. A receipt is required above ₹500.
